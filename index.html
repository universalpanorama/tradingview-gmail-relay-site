TradingView Gmail Relay

A private-use integration designed to read TradingView alert metadata from a designated Gmail account and route valid trading signals to an automated trading workflow.

How it works

Once enabled and authorised, the application is intended to inspect email metadata such as sender and subject, identify relevant TradingView strategy alerts, validate and process signal details, and pass authorised signals to a separate trading service. That service may use Airtable for operational state and Bitget for spot order execution.

This public website is informational only. It does not connect to Gmail, request Google sign-in, place trades or collect visitor account details.

Access and control

The Gmail connection requires explicit Google OAuth consent. The intended scope is gmail.metadata, which does not grant access to email bodies or attachments. Trading execution is handled separately and is subject to application configuration and testing.

For information about how Gmail data is handled, read the privacy policy.
